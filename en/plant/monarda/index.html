Bergamot / Bee Balm

Monarda spp.
CLimited feeding

Not a staple. Offer only occasionally as a minor addition.

Why this assessmentA specialist tortoise plant database classifies Monarda as feedable as part of a varied diet. It is called “bee balm” or “bergamot” in English, but it is an entirely different plant from Citrus bergamia, so the assessment must not be transferred on the basis of the name alone. It is also not a direct trial that sets an exact feeding proportion or frequency. Only plants correctly identified as Monarda and free of contamination are therefore used as a supplementary ingredient mixed with several foods.

How to feed

Do not use it as a staple. Build the diet on well-supported foods and add a small amount only occasionally.

Role of this plant One component of a varied mixed diet of plants
