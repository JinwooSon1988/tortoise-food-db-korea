# Pillajo 2020: locality-aware apparent-duplicate audit (2026-10-14)

Research-only review. No feeding-grade or safety conclusion follows from this audit.

## Scope and finding

Across Units 1–5, a naive same-source / tortoise / plant / part / preparation / wild-or-captive key flags eight pairs in the Pillajo Montesdeoca 2020 thesis. They are **not identical observations**: each pair has one record from the Santiago Island **arid zone** and one from the **humid zone**, with different reported frequencies and diet shares. Keep both records; do not count them as two independent studies. The source is one university thesis, and its method describes fecal seed/plant-remain analysis, **not direct visual feeding observations**.

The 90 transcribed named-taxon records comprise 61 arid-zone records and 29 humid-zone records. The source metadata says the original annexes contained 75 arid and 34 humid rows, of which 19 unnamed morphotypes were not transcribed. These denominators are distinct and must not be confused.

## Deduplication rule

For potential duplicate detection, include `locality` / habitat stratum (and any documented sampling stratum) in the comparison. Do not globally collapse records merely because source, tortoise, plant, part, preparation and wild/captive labels agree. Conversely, the distinct records must remain linked to **one source/study** for independent-study counts. Preserve each stratum's frequency and mean-share values without combining them or treating seed detection as proof of feeding safety.
