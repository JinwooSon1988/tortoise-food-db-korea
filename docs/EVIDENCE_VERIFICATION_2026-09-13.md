# Evidence verification — 2026-09-13

## 1. Bauer et al. Testudo husbandry paper DOI verification

The repository entry `testudo2018` is valid.

- Title: Nutrition and husbandry conditions of Palearctic tortoises (Testudo spp.) in captivity
- Authors: Thomas Bauer, Sven Reese, Petra Koelle
- Journal: Journal of Applied Animal Welfare Science
- DOI: `10.1080/10888705.2018.1453814`
- PMID: `29609473`
- Published online: 2018-04-02
- Final issue: 2019, 22(2):159-170

Interpretation rule: this is a captivity survey/husbandry paper, not plant-by-plant toxicity evidence. Its strongest useful dietary signal is that a summer diet dominated by grasses and weeds (>80% in the survey category described as optimum) was associated with better husbandry outcomes than diets with less grass/weed content. Do not use it to certify a specific plant as safe.

## 2. Iftime & Iftime 2012 — direct T. g. ibera field diet

Direct observation of more than 500 wild tortoises in Dobrogea, Romania over 2000-2010. Plant matter accounted for 96.5% of feeding observations. Table 1 records 25 plant taxa.

Relevant direct records:

- Taraxacum officinale — leaves and flowers — Moderate (5-10%)
- Sonchus sp. — leaves — Low (1-5%)
- Trifolium sp. — leaves and flowers — High (>10%)
- Medicago sp. — leaves — Moderate (5-10%)

Plantago is not listed in the paper's 25-plant table.

Interpretation rule: field observation frequency is not a captive ration recommendation.

## 3. Naumov et al. 2023 — south-western Bulgaria

For wild T. graeca ibera, the paper reports feeding on:

- Cichorium intybus
- Sedum rubens
- Sedum album
- Potentilla sp.
- Taraxacum sp.
- Medicago sp.

The same paper reports Plantago lanceolata under T. hermanni, not T. graeca ibera.

Interpretation rule: do not map Sedum rubens / S. album to Korean edible dolnamul (typically S. sarmentosum), and do not map Medicago sp. directly to retail alfalfa (M. sativa) without a species-level crosswalk.

## Release decision

- Medicago and Sedum records are now stored as research candidates, not public food verdict upgrades.
- Plantain remains at its existing Mediterranean-Testudo evidence level; no direct-Ibera upgrade was made.
- The database continues to separate `field_taxon` from Korean retail/common names.
