## Publication blockers needing special attention

1. **Taxonomic contradiction:** `lagarde_2003_primary-01` reports *Ceratocephalus falcatus* in a *Testudo horsfieldii* field study; its backbone name match conflicts with the source description. Do not auto-accept *Achillea falcata* or publish a species-specific verdict based on that match.
2. **Observed consumption does not equal safety:** `lagarde_2003_primary-02` reports Russian tortoises eating *Papaver pavoninum* flowers, while the source notes potentially toxic alkaloids in mammals. Preserve flower-specific, tortoise-specific context; do not convert to a universal food recommendation.
3. **Indirect and local-knowledge records:** source-reported local practices, provisioning and secondary citations must not be relabeled as controlled feeding trials.
4. **Seed-in-feces studies:** a plant identified from seeds in feces does not demonstrate which leaves, stems or flowers were eaten.
5. **Nutrition basis and part mismatch:** the nutrition layer must preserve wet/dry matter, plant part and preparation, and never automatically justify A/B/C/D grades.
6. **Data discrepancy:** the Unit 2 report flags a mismatch in the *Moorhouse-Gann* (2022) fecal morphology total. Hold affected transcription until checked against the original table.
7. **Species gaps:** the report explicitly notes missing plant-named evidence for *Chelonoidis denticulatus*, with limited new evidence for *Centrochelys sulcata*.
