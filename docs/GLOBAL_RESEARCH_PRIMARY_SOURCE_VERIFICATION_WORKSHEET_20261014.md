## Rules

1. Resolve multi-source citation strings into **individual primary works**. Reading one source never verifies the entire group.
2. Do not replace a species-specific observation with a general all-tortoise recommendation.
3. Do not infer leaf, pad, stem or flower consumption from the plant name alone.
4. Do not equate a recorded food plant, community seed mixture or avoidance observation with evidence of nutritional adequacy or safety.
5. Preserve the original research fields and attach a traceable review note; make any source-directness upgrade only after checking the original full text.
6. For contradictions, record both claims and the exact passages rather than silently choosing one.
