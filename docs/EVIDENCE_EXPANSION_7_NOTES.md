# Evidence expansion 7 — candidate-layer hardening

This batch does not promote additional foods to public-safe status. It improves the evidence pipeline by separating direct Ibera field taxa from Korean retail/common-name foods.

Key outcomes:

1. Medicago sp. is directly observed in wild T. g. ibera, but is not auto-mapped to retail alfalfa (Medicago sativa).
2. Sedum rubens and Sedum album are directly observed in wild T. g. ibera, but are not auto-mapped to Korean edible dolnamul (Sedum sarmentosum).
3. Plantago is not upgraded to direct-Ibera evidence because the checked Iftime & Iftime 2012 list lacks Plantago, while the Plantago lanceolata record in Naumov et al. 2023 belongs to T. hermanni.
4. The Bauer et al. Testudo husbandry DOI and PMID are verified; the paper remains genus-level husbandry evidence, not a plant-safety source.
5. CI now rejects candidate rows that would silently convert genus-level Ibera observations into retail-food approvals.
