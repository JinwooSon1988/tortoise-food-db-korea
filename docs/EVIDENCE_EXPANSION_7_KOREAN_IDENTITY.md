# Evidence expansion 7 — Korean identity resolution

## Scope
Korean common-name identity and tortoise feeding evidence are separate evidence axes. A botanical identity match does not itself establish feeding safety.

## 아욱
- BRIS/한국농업기술진흥원 and 한국민족문화대백과사전 identify cultivated Korean **아욱** as **Malva verticillata L.**
- Slimani et al. 2006 reported **Malva parviflora** among major plants in wild *Testudo graeca graeca* diet in Morocco.
- These are different species. Therefore the M. parviflora feeding evidence must not be transferred as species-direct evidence to Korean cultivated M. verticillata.
- Public verdict remains hold / evidence gap for M. verticillata-specific Testudo suitability.

## 방가지똥
- National Institute of Biological Resources national species list maps **방가지똥 = Sonchus oleraceus L.**
- **큰방가지똥 = Sonchus asper** is listed separately.
- Iftime & Iftime 2012 recorded **Sonchus sp.** leaves in wild *T. g. ibera* at Low (1–5%) observation frequency.
- Because the tortoise paper is genus-level, this does not prove the observed Sonchus was S. oleraceus.

## 토끼풀 / 클로버
- Korean references identify **토끼풀 = Trifolium repens L.** (white clover).
- BRIS/National Seed records also use 토끼풀(화이트클로버) for T. repens.
- Iftime & Iftime 2012 recorded **Trifolium sp.** leaves/flowers in wild *T. g. ibera* at High (>10%) observation frequency.
- This remains genus-level feeding evidence. The generic word **클로버** may refer to other Trifolium species, so only exact 토끼풀 is treated as T. repens identity evidence.

## Editorial rule
Keep these independent:
1. Korean identity confidence
2. exact-species tortoise evidence
3. genus-level tortoise evidence
4. specialist husbandry guidance
5. nutrient/toxicology evidence

Identity resolution alone never upgrades a feeding verdict.
