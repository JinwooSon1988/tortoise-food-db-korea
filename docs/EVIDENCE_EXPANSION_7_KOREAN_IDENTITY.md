# Evidence expansion 7 — Korean identity resolution

## Scope
This note separates Korean common/retail names from tortoise feeding evidence. Botanical identity does not by itself establish feeding safety.

## 아욱
- Korean agricultural/encyclopedic sources identify the cultivated vegetable **아욱** as **Malva verticillata L.**
- This resolves the Korean crop-name identity more tightly than the prior `Malva spp.` placeholder.
- The Morocco 2006 Testudo graeca graeca feeding study reported **Malva parviflora**, not M. verticillata.
- Therefore the direct wild-diet evidence for M. parviflora must **not** be transferred as species-direct evidence to Korean cultivated 아욱.
- Public verdict remains hold / evidence gap for M. verticillata-specific Testudo suitability.

Sources checked:
- BRIS / 한국농업기술진흥원 resource encyclopedia: 아욱 = Malva verticillata L.
- 한국민족문화대백과사전: 아욱 = Malva verticillata L.
- Slimani et al. 2006, Journal of Arid Environments: M. parviflora among major plants in wild T. g. graeca diet.

## 방가지똥
- National Institute of Biological Resources national species list maps **방가지똥 = Sonchus oleraceus L.**
- **큰방가지똥 = Sonchus asper** is a distinct Korean name/species.
- Iftime & Iftime 2012 recorded **Sonchus sp.** leaves in wild T. g. ibera at Low (1–5%) observation frequency.
- Because the tortoise paper is genus-level, it does not prove that the observed plant was S. oleraceus.
- The Korean name can be mapped more precisely, while the feeding evidence remains genus-direct rather than species-direct.

## 토끼풀 / 클로버
- Korean references identify **토끼풀 = Trifolium repens L.** (white clover).
- BRIS/National Seed sources also use 토끼풀(화이트클로버) for T. repens.
- Iftime & Iftime 2012 recorded **Trifolium sp.** leaves/flowers in wild T. g. ibera at High (>10%) observation frequency.
- This is genus-level feeding evidence and an observation-frequency class, not a captive ration percentage.
- The generic term **클로버** may refer to other Trifolium species, so only the exact Korean term 토끼풀 is treated as T. repens identity evidence.

## Editorial rule
A Korean common name can be botanically resolved without upgrading its feeding verdict. Evidence axes remain separate:
1. Korean identity confidence
2. plant-specific tortoise evidence
3. genus-level tortoise evidence
4. specialist husbandry guidance
5. nutrient/toxicology evidence
