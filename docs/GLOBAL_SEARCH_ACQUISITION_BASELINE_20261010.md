# Global search acquisition baseline — 2026-10-10

## Verified in repository
- Sitemap contains 376 canonical URL entries, including Korean and English content.
- English detail examples `/en/plant/dandelion/` and `/en/plant/chicory/` use query-intent titles ("Can tortoises eat …?"), English meta descriptions, and self-referencing canonical URLs.
- The English `/en/all-plants/` noindex redirect is excluded from the sitemap after PR #246.
- CI on the SEO PR passed 30/30 before merge.

## Not yet verified
- Google/Bing indexing coverage, impressions, clicks, CTR, or rankings by country.
- GitHub Pages post-merge deployment propagation.
- Google Search Console / Bing Webmaster Tools ownership and submissions.
- Actual search demand volumes and regional keyword difficulty.

## Acquisition priorities
1. Verify public sitemap and page canonical/hreflang after deployment.
2. Confirm Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools properties, submit the sitemap, and inspect indexing reports; do not report submission without evidence.
3. Use Search Console country/query/page performance to prioritize English pages. Avoid fabricating search-volume figures.
4. Preserve one plant identity per canonical page and keep species-specific caveats in the content rather than creating thin species duplicates.
5. Improve internal linking from high-intent guides to relevant plant detail pages; avoid keyword stuffing and unsupported safety claims.
6. Consider further locales only after native-quality translation and evidence parity are feasible.

## Success measures
- Indexed / eligible pages by locale
- Organic impressions, clicks and CTR by country and query
- Search entry pages and engagement
- Citation/referral links from relevant tortoise care resources

This document is a planning and verification baseline, not evidence of traffic growth.
