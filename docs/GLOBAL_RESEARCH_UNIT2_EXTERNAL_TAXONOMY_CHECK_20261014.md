## `lagarde_2003_primary-04` — `Roemeria sp.`

- Source: Lagarde et al. (2003) tortoise feeding record, `Testudo horsfieldii`; original transcription `Roemeria sp.`; plant part `not stated`.
- External reference: Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, Plants of the World Online (POWO), *Roemeria* Medik., https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:31714-1 (accessed 2026-10-14).
- Kew lists *Roemeria* Medik. as an **accepted genus** in Papaveraceae, with multiple accepted species.
- **Resolution:** the genus name itself is botanically recognized, but the source only specifies `sp.` and cannot be assigned to any particular *Roemeria* species. Its recorded `homonyms_in_backbone_author_not_given_needs_review` status remains open pending original-source taxonomic context and author citation.
- **Feeding implication:** no edible part is specified and a wild consumption report is not a safety verdict. Do not assign a feeding grade or extend the record to all *Roemeria* species or all tortoises.

- **Resolution:** this does not independently prove the species identity in the original field study; retain the conflict, original name, and `plant_accepted_name: null`. Do not substitute *Achillea* or promote the record to a feeding recommendation.
