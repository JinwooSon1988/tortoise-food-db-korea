# Evidence expansion 6 — direct Ibera diet evidence

## Scope
This batch replaces secondary-only interpretation with primary field observations wherever possible for foods already prominent in the Korean tortoise-feeding workflow.

## Primary evidence added

### Iftime & Iftime 2012 — Dobrogea, Romania
- Taxon: wild `Testudo graeca ibera`
- Method: direct feeding observations over a long-term field study
- Plant matter: 96.5% of feeding observations
- 25 plant species recorded
- `Taraxacum officinale`: leaves/flowers, Moderate (5–10%)
- `Sonchus sp.`: leaves, Low (1–5%)
- `Trifolium sp.`: leaves/flowers, High (>10%)
- DOI: `10.13128/Acta_Herpetol-9800`

These are field-observation frequency classes, not captive-diet percentages.

### Mitrevichin et al. 2023 — south-western Bulgaria
- Taxon: `Testudo graeca ibera`
- Directly observed plant use includes `Cichorium intybus` and `Taraxacum sp.`
- The study does not provide plant-specific long-term captive feeding ratios.
- ZooNotes 228: 1–4
- DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.12807900`

## Assessment changes
- Dandelion: retained green mixed-diet status; confidence strengthened to `B+` because direct Ibera observations now exist from two regions. No captive percentage is inferred from wild frequency.
- Sow-thistle: retained yellow mixed-diet support; confidence strengthened to `B` and primary Ibera evidence now supersedes reliance on a later species-account summary.
- Clover: retained yellow limited-mixed-diet status; confidence strengthened to `B`. Direct Ibera use is strong, but `Trifolium` species differences and specialist moderation guidance prevent a blanket high-frequency captive recommendation.
- Chicory: moved from tortoise-general applicability to Mediterranean Testudo applicability at `B-`, because `Cichorium intybus` was directly observed in wild `T. g. ibera`. It remains a limited mixed-diet item rather than a staple.
- Plantain: unchanged. The reviewed direct Ibera sources did not provide a sufficiently clean `Plantago` observation for this batch, so no evidence grade inflation was applied.

## Safety rule retained
Wild consumption ≠ unrestricted captive feeding. Botanical identity, contamination risk, plant part, overall diet diversity, and species-specific husbandry context remain separate decision layers.
