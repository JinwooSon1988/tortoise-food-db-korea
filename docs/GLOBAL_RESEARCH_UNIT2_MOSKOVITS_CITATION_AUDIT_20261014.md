# Unit 2 De la Ossa / Moskovits reference correction — 2026-10-14

## Verified publication chain

The De la Ossa et al. (2009) article, page 158, prints `Duquetia surinamensis` and `Bagassa guianenesis` in a list **attributed to MOSKOVITS (1981)**, not to its own captive flower experiment.

- Source PDF, p. 158: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/3268961.pdf
- The same 2009 article's references (p. 166) list: `MOSKOVITS, D.K. 1981. Sexual dimorphism and population estimates ... Herpetologica 44(2):237–256.` This citation is **bibliographically inconsistent**.
- Multiple independent bibliographies identify the *sexual dimorphism* publication as **Moskovits (1988), Herpetologica 44(2):209–217**, not 1981, 237–256:
  - IUCN tortoise bibliography: https://portals.iucn.org/library/sites/library/files/documents/SSC-OP-005.pdf
  - RepFocus bibliography: https://repfocus.dk/Chelonoidis_bibliography.html
- A **separate, directly relevant diet paper** exists: Moskovits, D. K. & Bjorndal, K. A. (1990), *Diet and food preferences of the tortoises Geochelone carbonaria and G. denticulata in northwestern Brazil*, **Herpetologica 46(2):207–218**. Bibliographic reference: https://repfocus.dk/Chelonoidis_bibliography.html ; abstract: https://documentsdelivered.com/source/017/847/017847035.php
- That 1990 paper's abstract describes free-ranging feeding observations and scat examinations; fruit and flower seasonality. **The full text and plant-specific names in that paper were not independently read** for this audit.

## Interpretation

The 2009 reference `Moskovits (1981)` should be flagged as a **misdated/miscited or unresolved reference**, not silently normalized to either the 1988 population paper or the 1990 diet paper. The 1990 diet paper is a **plausible source to check**, not proven to be the exact source for the five species named on page 158.

For `delaossa_2009-20` and `delaossa_2009-24`:

- 2009 secondary source **verbatim spelling verified**: yes.
- Original wild-diet primary paper verified: **no**.
- Accepted botanical name verified: **no**.
- Feeding part: **not stated**.
- Species scope: *Chelonoidis carbonarius* (printed in older combination *Geochelone carbonaria*), not all tortoises.
- Safety/feeding grade: **no inference**.
