# Academic physiology inference boundary v5.6

Two source classes must remain separate from direct wild-feeding evidence.

- PMID 17988349 is a controlled mineral-digestibility feeding study in eight adult *Testudo hermanni*. It supports mineral-physiology context, not *Testudo graeca ibera* requirements and not named-plant safety/frequency.
- PMID 20630020 is a clinical blood-chemistry study of 25 captive *Testudo* tortoises. *Testudo graeca ibera* is included in the sampled taxa, but this is not a feeding trial and does not isolate an Ibera dietary requirement.

Therefore `Ibera included in study sample` must never be transformed into `direct Ibera feeding evidence`. Direct plant evidence requires an observation or feeding study that actually links the relevant taxon to the relevant plant identity/part.

Experimental mixed-diet Ca:P values and blood chemistry values must not be converted into universal named-plant feeding ratios or public feeding verdicts. `feeding_verdict_use` remains false.
