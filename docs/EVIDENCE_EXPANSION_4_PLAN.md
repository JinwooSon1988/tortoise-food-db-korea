# Evidence expansion 4

Targets: chicory (`Cichorium intybus`) and mulberry leaf (`Morus spp.`).

Policy:
- Tortoise Table is treated as specialist tortoise-feeding guidance, not peer-reviewed species-direct evidence.
- Chicory leaves/flowers may be represented as a moderation candidate based on the specialist plant database, but roots remain excluded from that positive scope.
- Mulberry leaves may be represented as part of a varied tortoise diet, but fruit guidance is not generalized to Mediterranean Testudo.
- Neither item is promoted to a Mediterranean-Testudo-specific verdict without direct evidence.
- Existing chemistry/nutrition papers remain contextual and do not independently establish tortoise safety.
