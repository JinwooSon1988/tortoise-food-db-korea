# Academic physiology source verification v5.6

This verification note records source-level boundaries before canonical evidence ingestion. It does not change public feeding verdicts, feeding frequency, diet percentages, or evidence grades.

## PMID 17988349 — Liesegang, Hatt & Wanner (2007)

- Title: *Influence of different dietary calcium levels on the digestibility of Ca, Mg and P in Hermann's tortoises (Testudo hermanni)*
- Journal: Journal of Animal Physiology and Animal Nutrition 91(11-12):459-464
- DOI: `10.1111/j.1439-0396.2007.00676.x`
- Taxon: `Testudo hermanni`
- Design: controlled mineral-digestibility feeding study; eight adult Hermann's tortoises.
- Diet context: mixed vegetables/herbs with different calcium-carbonate supplementation.
- Supports: calcium, magnesium and phosphorus apparent-digestibility physiology under the tested diets; mineral-nutrition context for Testudo.
- Does **not** support: safety of any named plant; an Ibera-specific feeding prescription; a universal target Ca:P ratio for every meal; daily/unlimited feeding of any plant.

## PMID 20630020 — Eatwell (2010)

- Title: *Calcium and phosphorus values and their derivatives in captive tortoises (Testudo species)*
- Journal: Journal of Small Animal Practice 51(9):472-475
- DOI: `10.1111/j.1748-5827.2010.00963.x`
- Taxa: 25 clinically healthy captive tortoises including `Testudo graeca ibera`, `T. hermanni boettgeri`, `T. marginata`, and `T. horsfieldi`.
- Design: post-hibernation blood sampling before reproductive activity; total calcium, ionised calcium and phosphorus measured.
- Supports: clinical/reference physiology context for calcium/phosphorus interpretation in captive Testudo; direct inclusion of T. g. ibera in the sampled population.
- Does **not** support: a feeding trial; plant-specific suitability; dietary calcium requirements; named-plant feeding frequency or percentage; attribution of the measured values to a particular diet.

## Iftime & Iftime (2012) — direct Ibera field evidence

- Title: *Long term observations on the alimentation of wild Eastern Greek Tortoises Testudo graeca ibera ... in Dobrogea, Romania*
- Acta Herpetologica 7(1):105-110
- DOI: `10.13128/Acta_Herpetol-9800`
- Method: direct observation of wild `Testudo graeca ibera`.
- Published abstract reports over 95% plant matter and 25 consumed plant species.
- This remains a direct wild-feeding source, but wild occurrence must not be converted into captive feeding percentages, daily feeding, or unlimited-safety claims.

## Canonical ingestion rule

`17988349` belongs at Testudo/general mineral-digestibility context, not plant rows. `20630020` is unusually valuable because the sample explicitly includes `T. g. ibera`, but its directness is physiological/clinical rather than dietary. Neither source may raise a plant feeding verdict or plant-specific evidence grade. The Iftime source may support mapped direct wild-occurrence rows only where plant taxon scope is preserved.
