# Evidence expansion 8 — Medicago / alfalfa and Korean dolnamul

Reviewed: 2026-09-14

## Goal

Strengthen two high-value Korean food records without collapsing genus-level wild-diet evidence into exact-species feeding claims.

## Alfalfa / 자주개자리 — `Medicago sativa`

### Identity
- Korean agricultural sources identify alfalfa / 자주개자리 as `Medicago sativa`.
- This resolves the Korean crop identity but does **not** change the taxonomic resolution of the Ibera field studies.

### Ibera evidence
- Iftime & Iftime 2012 directly observed wild `Testudo graeca ibera` feeding on `Medicago sp.` in Dobrogea, Romania. The paper classifies the observation frequency as Moderate (5–10%). This is a wild observation-frequency class, not a captive diet percentage.
- Mitrevichin et al. 2023 independently observed `T. g. ibera` feeding on `Medicago sp.` in south-western Bulgaria.
- The 2023 eastern `T. graeca` species account summarizes `Medicago` among frequently consumed plant groups in Romanian Ibera.
- A separate peer-reviewed `T. graeca` paper mentions alfalfa (`Medicago sativa`) as part of the species' wild plant diet while citing earlier diet literature. It is context evidence, not an Ibera direct feeding trial.

### Public assessment
- `🟡 제한적 혼합급여 근거`
- Confidence: `B-`
- Reason for not assigning a green/staple verdict: direct Ibera observations are genus-level `Medicago sp.`, not confirmed `M. sativa`, and no source supplies a validated captive percentage or long-term single-food prescription.

## 돌나물 — `Sedum sarmentosum`

### Identity
- Korean biodiversity/agricultural sources identify 돌나물 as `Sedum sarmentosum Bunge`.

### Tortoise evidence
- Mitrevichin et al. 2023 directly observed wild `T. g. ibera` feeding on `Sedum rubens` and `Sedum album`.
- Those are **not** `Sedum sarmentosum`; therefore the field observation must not be promoted to exact-species direct evidence for Korean 돌나물.
- The Tortoise Table classifies most `Sedum spp.` as usable within a varied tortoise diet, warns that excessive succulent intake may loosen stools, and specifically excludes `Sedum acre`.

### Public assessment
- `🟡 제한적 보조식 근거`
- Scope: `Tortoise_general`, not `Mediterranean_Testudo`.
- Confidence: `C-`.
- This preserves the exact-species gap while still surfacing relevant genus-level field and specialist evidence.

## DOI verification cleanup

`testudo2018` was checked against PubMed. DOI `10.1080/10888705.2018.1453814` and PMID `29609473` are valid. The record now distinguishes the 2019 journal issue from the 2018 online publication date.

## Editorial invariants

1. Wild observation frequency is never converted to captive feeding percentage.
2. Genus-level evidence is never silently upgraded to exact-species evidence.
3. Korean plant identity and tortoise feeding suitability are separate evidence axes.
4. A yellow verdict means limited/supporting evidence, not unlimited feeding or a staple-food recommendation.
5. Absence of exact-species evidence is surfaced rather than filled by assumption.
