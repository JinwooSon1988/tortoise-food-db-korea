# Evidence expansion 5 — direct Ibera wild-diet evidence

## Source strengthened
Türkozan et al. (2023), *Testudo graeca* eastern subspecies clades, Chelonian Research Monographs No. 5, Account 120, DOI 10.3854/crm.5.120.eastern.graeca.v1.2023.

The species account summarizes a roughly 10-year northeast Romania dataset of about 500 wild *T. g. ibera*. The diet was reported as 96.5% herbivorous. Young leaves of *Trifolium* and *Sonchus* were among the most frequently consumed plant materials.

## Public assessment changes
- `sowthistle` / *Sonchus* spp.: retain 🟡 mixed-diet support, but strengthen applicability to direct wild *T. g. ibera* evidence. Confidence moves from C+ to B-.
- `clover` / *Trifolium* spp.: retain 🟡 limited mixed-diet status because Tortoise Trust still advises moderation for white clover, while adding direct wild *T. g. ibera* evidence. Confidence moves from C to B-.

Wild consumption is not interpreted as a captive dose, exclusive diet, or unlimited-feeding recommendation.

## Mallow identity gate remains
The 2006 Morocco study directly identified *Malva parviflora* as a major, preferred wild food of *T. g. graeca*. This does **not** justify mapping a Korean retail product sold simply as `아욱` to *M. parviflora*. The generic `mallow` page therefore remains ⚪ when the retail botanical identity is unresolved. Exact botanical identity and feeding assessment remain separate layers.
