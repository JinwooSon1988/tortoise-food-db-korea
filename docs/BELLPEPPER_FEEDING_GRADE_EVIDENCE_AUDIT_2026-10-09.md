# Bell pepper feeding assessment: independent evidence audit (2026-10-09)

Status: **editorial review required; no feeding grade changed**. This file documents an evidence-logic concern independently of the nutrition-data expansion work.

## Repository evidence checked
- `data/public_assessments.json` contains `bellpepper`, `verdict: limited_supplement`, `confidence: C`.
- Its rationale explicitly recognizes that The Tortoise Table advises not feeding bell/sweet pepper fruit and that the cited pepper chemistry study is **not** a tortoise feeding-safety study.
- The existing rationale nevertheless concludes that limited supplementary feeding is more justified than the specialist's avoid-feeding guidance. The inferential bridge from "no proven toxicity threshold" to "limited feeding is advisable" is not established by those cited sources.
- `data/bellpepper_feeding_evidence_reassessment_20261008.json` already records this conflict and holds off on automatic regrading.

## Editorial distinction to preserve
1. **Not recommended as a food** is not equivalent to **proven acutely toxic**.
2. Chemical composition, capsaicinoid variation and USDA Ca:P measurements are not tortoise feeding trials and do not independently establish a safe feeding frequency.
3. Fruits and vegetative plant parts must not share a single undifferentiated safety claim.
4. A source exception for fruit-eating tortoise species cannot be generalized to all tortoises or to Mediterranean Testudo.

## Resolution criteria
- Recheck the current original specialist source for exact taxon, fruit/leaf distinction, rationale and any species-specific exception; capture access date and exact page URL.
- Check for primary veterinary, toxicology or controlled feeding evidence relevant to tortoise species and Capsicum annuum fruit. Record negative search results as search limitations, not proof of safety or toxicity.
- Compare directness of evidence: tortoise-specific feeding guidance vs food chemistry. Do not count nutrition values as affirmative feeding-safety evidence.
- If a revised public recommendation is warranted, update the assessment and generated page consistently, with a plain-language explanation that differentiates dietary suitability from poisoning risk.
- Add a QA check that flags when an avoid-feeding specialist source is used to justify a positive feeding verdict without an independent affirmative basis.
